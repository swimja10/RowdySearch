# RowdySearch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

RowdySearch is a Chrome extension that shows UTSA professor information (past syllabi, Rate My Professors ratings, and historical grade distributions) while you register for classes. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by UTSA, Rate My Professors, or Simple Syllabus.

## What RowdySearch reads

On UTSA's Schedule Planner and Registration pages, RowdySearch reads the class tables shown on the page (course, section, instructor, CRN, and term) so it knows which professor to look up. On the Registration page it makes one request to UTSA's own registration system to get a section's instructor name, because that table does not show it.

If you click the RowdySearch toolbar icon on another website, the extension adds its lookup sidebar to that tab. It does not read or use that page's content.

## What RowdySearch collects

Nothing. The information above is used only inside your browser, at the moment you look up a professor. RowdySearch does not save it, and does not send it to the developer or to any third party. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

All professor data is bundled inside the extension. The grade distributions were obtained from UTSA through a Texas Public Information Act request.

## Links to other sites

RowdySearch links to professor pages on Rate My Professors and to syllabi on Simple Syllabus. Opening those links takes you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
