# RowdySearch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

RowdySearch is a Chrome extension that shows UTSA professor information (past syllabi, Bluebook course evaluations, Rate My Professors ratings, and historical grade distributions) while you register for classes. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by UTSA, Rate My Professors, or Simple Syllabus.

## What RowdySearch reads

On UTSA's Schedule Planner and Registration pages, RowdySearch reads the class tables shown on the page (course, section, instructor, CRN, and term) so it knows which professor to look up. On the Registration page it makes one request to UTSA's own registration system to get a section's instructor name, because that table does not show it.

If you click the RowdySearch toolbar icon on another website, the extension adds its lookup sidebar to that tab. It does not read or use that page's content.

## What RowdySearch collects

Nothing. The information above is used only inside your browser, at the moment you look up a professor. RowdySearch does not save it, and does not send it to the developer or to any third party. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

All professor data is bundled inside the extension. The grade distributions were obtained from UTSA through a Texas Public Information Act request. The course evaluations are UTSA's public Bluebook results.

## Links to other sites

RowdySearch links to professor pages on Rate My Professors, to syllabi on Simple Syllabus, and to its own page on the Chrome Web Store. Its Share buttons open the site or app you pick (like X, Reddit, or GroupMe) with a message about RowdySearch ready to send; for apps without a share link, the button puts that message on your clipboard instead. Nothing is posted unless you send it yourself. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
